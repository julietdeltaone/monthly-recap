A note on the physical numbers. The January plan set the AFT goal at a 90-point minimum per event — 230 lb deadlift, 43 push-ups, a 2:25 plank, 15:50 two-mile run. Later months' dashboards tracked toward a visibly higher personal bar (350 lb, 61 reps, 3:35, 16:36) without ever renaming it as a change. That looks like a drift toward the AFT’s 100-point max scoring rather than a new decision, but it was never written down as one. This scorecard uses the actual January numbers.
