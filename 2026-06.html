Career: where this actually stands
Plainly: the part JD controlled got done. The 368 moved through his chain and reached the Air Force recruiter before its own deadline. The part he does not control is what happens now. No recruiter has been assigned yet, and there is no way to know if that takes another week or another month. The honest read is that the AFOQT date (Sep 25) is more reliable than the recruiter timeline right now, since it does not depend on anyone getting back to him. The Clarkson interview is the wildcard. It was not the plan A path, but a same-field admin role at a university he already knows would be a reasonable fallback if OTS timing drags into next year.
Academic: a genuinely clean result
A 4.000 term GPA is not a number that needs much interpretation. It closes the spring cleanly and pushes the cumulative to 3.917. The open question is whether that pace holds once Fall classes stack back up against whatever the OTS process demands in September and October. Worth watching: if the AFOQT and interview prep land in the same window as the first weeks of a new term, something will get less attention than it deserves. That is a scheduling risk, not a performance one.
Physical: the number that matters moved
No new maxes on the barbell lifts this month, and that is fine. The number that actually moved was pace: both outdoor runs came back roughly a minute per mile faster than their May equivalents. That is the metric tied directly to the AFT two-mile run, and it is trending the right way before the test even mattered on a calendar. The AFT moving to October is a real gift here. It buys another three months to keep building that same trend instead of peaking too early.
Spiritual: a theme that lined up with the month
Three sermons, three angles on the same question: how do you trust God when you cannot see the outcome yet. That is not a coincidence worth overreading, but it is worth naming, since it tracked almost exactly against a month spent waiting to hear back from a recruiter. Whether that connection was intentional on Brad’s part or not, it landed at a useful time.
Possible next steps

1. Follow up with the AF recruiter directly if there is still no contact by mid-July. The 368 being approved does not guarantee anyone is actively working the file.

2. Start AFOQT prep now rather than waiting for a scheduled date to force it. Sep 25 sounds far away in July, it will not in September.

3. If the Clarkson interview turns into an offer, that decision will need a real side-by-side against the OTS timeline, not a reflexive yes or no.

4. Keep the outdoor running cadence through the summer heat. The pace gains are real and worth protecting through October.
