07

Reflections

August asked for everything and paid it back in the ledger. Annual Training plus the AFOQT plus two weddings is not a sustainable cadence — but it was a clarifying one. The through-line of the month: preparation is the whole game. The camera overheated because heat management wasn’t planned for; Quant 6 happened because math wasn’t drilled like the job depended on it. Both get fixed the same way — systems, not willpower.

Financial discipline held under the strangest income month of the year: windfalls came in, the EUC went out, and the net still printed +$4,065.06. That’s the habit working.

“Backup bodies, cold cameras, drilled math. August’s tuition, paid in full.”

Looking ahead to September: the pipeline shoots (Foley and Bryson on the 26th), the MBA semester finding its rhythm, and the career thread narrowing — OTS is the clear live path, the 27OTS01R board targets January 2027, and the AFT lands October 23–25 at home station. Clarkson semester kickoff brings the Apps Script tools and a possible Claude org license. September’s job: convert the pipeline, drill Quant, and don’t miss the aisle.
