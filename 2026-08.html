Annual Training plus the AFOQT plus two weddings is not a sustainable cadence — but it was a clarifying one.

b.Preparation is the whole game

The camera overheated because heat management wasn’t planned for; Quant 6 happened because math wasn’t drilled like the job depended on it. Both get fixed the same way — systems, not willpower.

Financial discipline held under the strangest income month of the year: windfalls came in, the EUC went out, and the net still printed +$4,065.06. That’s the habit working.
