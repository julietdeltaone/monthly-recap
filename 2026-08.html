Conviction series · James 5:16

The season’s theme: a season of conviction — the Holy Spirit surfacing things He hasn’t convicted of before, things previously easy to excuse. The core distinction drawn was conviction vs. condemnation: conviction responded to promptly draws you toward God; left sitting, it curdles into condemnation, which pulls you away from His presence and wrecks relationships in the process.

The illustration was the pastor’s own mother — a woman who loved the Lord, but whose unaddressed jealousy, confronted and rejected for decades, turned into church-hopping and burned bridges, even with her sons. The application, individual and corporate: respond to conviction right away — and let the body of Christ close enough to speak into your life.

07Reflections

August asked for everything and paid it back in the ledger.

“Backup bodies, cold cameras, drilled math. August’s tuition, paid in full.”

a.A clarifying cadence

Annual Training plus the AFOQT plus two weddings is not a sustainable cadence — but it was a clarifying one. Sixteen days in the field, an officer-qualifying exam, and two clients’ biggest days, back to back to back. The month proved the ceiling is higher than the routine suggests.

b.The only AT

Since the ETS is June 2027 — before the next AT cycle — Fort Drum was the one and only annual training with the unit. S1 shop, a surprise rotation to the TAC as RTO, battle drills at dawn, ten days in tents, one legendary LMTV shower run. It went about how it was expected to go, which is its own kind of good.

c.Preparation is the whole game

The camera overheated because heat management wasn’t planned for; Quant 6 happened because math wasn’t drilled like the job depended on it. Both get fixed the same way — systems, not willpower. The R6 II is on the shopping list; the math block is on the calendar.

d.Discipline held

Financial discipline held under the strangest income month of the year: windfalls came in, the EUC went out, and the net still printed +$4,121.80. That’s the habit working. $5,000 swept to savings on the 28th anyway.

e.Field notes — the EUC

Picked up the InMotion V9 to upgrade the 2018 Boosted board — the board only does smooth asphalt, and the whole point was getting down the gravel driveway. So far: good but frustrating. Roads and light dirt are fine; stopping, remounting, and loose gravel are all still rough. The learning curve is the project.

f.September

Convert the pipeline (Foley and Bryson on the 26th), drill Quant, find the MBA rhythm. OTS is the clear live path — board 27OTS01R targets January 2027, AFT lands Oct 23–25. Don’t miss the aisle.
